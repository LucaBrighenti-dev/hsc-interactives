Privacy

This site is a free set of teaching simulations for NSW Stage 6 Extension Maths, Physics and Engineering Studies, built by Luca Brighenti for UNSW Teach the Teachers 2026. It is not run by NESA, the NSW Department of Education or UNSW.

01What we keep

  • Teachers who sign in with Microsoft, Google or an email link: your email address, the name your sign-in provider shares, and anything you choose to add (a display name, your school, the subjects you teach).
  • Things you make: saved scenarios, lesson playlists, requests for new interactives, and your votes on requests.
  • Access-code sessions (for classes): no name or email at all. The session is anonymous.

02What we never collect

  • Student personal details. Students should use the access code their teacher gives them, not a personal Google or Microsoft account.
  • Analytics, advertising trackers or third-party cookies. The pages load nothing from other websites.

03Who can see it

Only you can see your profile, saved scenarios and playlists. Requests for new interactives and their vote counts are visible to everyone who is signed in, without your name. Sharing a scenario link shares the simulation settings only, never your account.

04Where it is stored

Accounts and saved items are stored with Supabase in its Sydney (Australia) region. Your browser keeps your sign-in session and your stage theme preference in local storage on your device.

05Deleting your data

You can delete your account from your dashboard at any time. This permanently removes your profile, saved scenarios, playlists, requests and votes.

06Contact

Questions about privacy: Luca.Brighenti1@outlook.com.
