Each line is one spec: its id, what a student should get from it, and the key acceptance numbers (every one has its formula and hand working in the spec, and was re-checked with a short `node` calculation). All presets use **g = 10 m/s²** where g appears, shown on stage. Moments are clockwise positive. Tension and compression always use a line style and a T/C label as well as colour.

**How to approve:** skim the lines below, open two or three specs, and check that the learning goal fits the syllabus, the ranges suit a classroom, the numbers look right and the prompts make students think. Then tick **Gate B / eng** in `docs/STATUS.md` (or tell the orchestrator).

## P0

- **eng-flight-forces** (aero / flight-forces). Steady flight balances L = W cos γ and T = D + W sin γ; lift needs speed² and angle of attack, and the wing stalls past its critical angle. 1000 kg, S = 16 m², sea level: at 40 m/s L = 10 000 N, C_L = 0.6378, α = 3.99°, D = T = 789.3 N, L/D = 12.67; 5° climb at 45 m/s needs T = 1717 N; V_s = 23.52 m/s (27.30 m/s at 3000 m); (L/D)max = 12.91 at 36.30 m/s, best glide 4.43°; at 20 m/s only 7232 N of lift (stalled).
- **eng-truss-solver** (statics / truss). Method of joints: reactions from the whole truss, then ΣFx = ΣFy = 0 joint by joint; m + r = 2j for determinacy. Equilateral, 10 kN apex: inclined −5.774 kN (C), base +2.887 kN (T), reactions 5 kN; 4-panel Warren 12 m × 3 m, 20 kN mid-span: top chord GH −20 kN, bottom BC +15 kN, end diagonal −11.18 kN; 3-4-5 bracket, 12 kN: tie +16 kN, strut −20 kN; square frame m + r − 2j = −1, braced +1; custom truss with a zero-force member.

## P1

- **eng-beam-lab** (statics / beam). Reactions, SFD and BMD, maximum moment where V = 0, σ = My/I. 100 × 300 mm (I = 225 × 10⁶ mm⁴): 6 m central 20 kN → 30 kN·m, 20 MPa; UDL 10 kN/m → 45 kN·m, 30 MPa; 30 kN at 2 m → R 20/10 kN, 40 kN·m; cantilever 3 m, 10 kN → −30 kN·m, wall moment 30 kN·m; 8 m with 20 kN + 5 kN/m → R 35/25 kN, 62.5 kN·m at 3 m.
- **eng-braking-energy** (particle2d / braking). Braking distance v²/(2μg) and kinetic energy into brake heat. 20 m/s, μ = 0.7: 28.57 m in 2.857 s, friction force 10.5 kN; 1500 kg → 300 kJ, all into 30 kg of iron rotors (c = 449 J/(kg·K), CRC Handbook) → 22.3 K; with a 1.5 s reaction 58.57 m; 40 m/s → 114.3 m; wet μ = 0.4 → 50 m.
- **eng-force-sandbox** (statics / concurrent). Components, resultant, equilibrant, force polygon and the three-force rule. 30 N ⟂ 40 N → 50 N at 53.13°; three 100 N at 120° → 0; 50 N @ 30°, 80 N @ 135°, 40 N @ −110° → 51.58 N at 121.50°; 3-4-5 boom with 6 kN at the end → T = 10 kN, pin 8 kN at 0°; at mid-span → T = 5 kN, pin 5 kN at 36.87°.
- **eng-materials-selection** (materials / selection). Specific strength σ/ρ decides the lightest tie (m = nFLρ/σ). 50 kN, n = 1.5, 2 m: 2024-T3 124.1 kN·m/kg, 1.209 kg; Ti-6Al-4V 205.9, 0.729 kg; high-tensile (4340) steel 109.8, 1.366 kg; CFRP (0/90 fabric) 375, 0.400 kg; Grade 300 structural steel 38.2, 3.925 kg. Sources: ASM Handbooks, AZoM, Performance Composites, AS/NZS 3679.1.
- **eng-modulation-lab** (wave / modulation). AM, FM and PM; modulation index, side frequencies and bandwidth. AM f_c 20 Hz, f_m 1 Hz, m 0.5: 19/21 Hz, B = 2 Hz; FM Δf = 3 Hz: β = 3, Carson B = 8 Hz, frequency 23 Hz at t = 0.25 s and 17 Hz at 0.75 s; PM Δφ = 1.5 rad at 2 Hz: index 1.5, B = 10 Hz, 23 Hz at t = 0; FSK square message: 24 Hz and 16 Hz tones.
- **eng-moments** (statics / moments). Clockwise-positive moments; reactions from ΣM = 0 and ΣFy = 0; moments balance about any point. 6 m beam, 10 kN at 2 m, 20 kN at 4.5 m: R_A 11.67, R_B 18.33 kN, 110 kN·m each way about A, 65 each way about 3 m; anticlockwise convention flips the sign only; overhanging beam R_A = R_B = 13 kN; see-saw 6/3 kN.
- **eng-orbits-gps** (particle2d / orbit-gps). LEO, MEO, GEO periods and speeds over one sidereal day; GPS ranges from travel time and the effect of clock error. Altitudes from the equatorial radius 6378.137 km (requested of particle2d): LEO 400 km 92.56 min, 7669 m/s; GPS (half a sidereal day) 11.967 h, altitude 20 184 km, 3874 m/s; GEO 23.934 h, altitude 35 786 km, 3075 m/s; signal from the highest GPS satellite 68.99 ms (receiver at 30°); 1 μs clock error = 299.79 m of range.
- **eng-orthographic-trainer** (space3d / orthographic). Front, top and right views of simple parts in third-angle projection (AS 1100), against first angle, with hidden lines. Step block 60 × 30 × 40: part volume 54 000 mm³, view areas 1800/1800/1200 mm², top view above and right view to the right (third) or swapped (first); slotted, chamfered and drilled blocks with volumes 64 000, 63 000 and 41 716.8 mm³.
- **eng-pascal-hydraulics** (custom). Pascal's principle F₂ = F₁A₂/A₁, with volume conservation and work in = work out. 200 N on Ø20 into Ø100: p = 0.637 MPa, F₂ = 5000 N, travel 100 → 4 mm, 20 J each; 400 kg needs 160 N; 80% → 4000 N; aircraft actuator at 20.7 MPa, Ø50/Ø20: extend 40.64 kN, retract 34.14 kN, 392.7 mL per 200 mm.
- **eng-pitot-altimeter** (aero / pitot-static). Airspeed from p_t − p_s = ½ρV²; altitude from static pressure and why the altimeter must be set to QNH. Sea level 40 m/s: Δp = 980 Pa; 3000 m: ρ = 0.9091 kg/m³, IAS 51.69 m/s for 60 m/s true; 11 000 m: 216.65 K, 22 632 Pa; set 10 hPa low reads −83.6 m; high to low unset reads 83.6 m high.
- **eng-simple-machines** (custom). VR from geometry, MA = load/effort, η = MA/VR, and work in against work out. Class 1 lever 1.2/0.3 m: VR = MA = 4, 250 N lifts 100 kg; 4-strand pulley at 80%: MA 3.2, 625 N, 1250 J in for 1000 J out; 12 → 36 gears at 95%: 100 rpm, 28.5 N·m; screw jack 5 mm pitch, 300 mm handle at 30%: VR 377, effort 132.6 N for 1500 kg.
- **eng-tensile-test** (materials / tensile). Load–extension to stress–strain; E, yield (0.2% proof), UTS, elongation and toughness for ductile and brittle materials. Grade 300 structural steel Ø10: A = 78.54 mm², E 200 GPa, yield 300 MPa, UTS 440 MPa, max load 34.56 kN, 22%; Ø20 → 138.2 kN, same properties; alumina toughness ½σε = 0.1915 MJ/m³; copper E 117 GPa, proof 69 MPa; 12.5 × 3 mm 2024-T3 bar → 18.11 kN. Sources: AS/NZS 3679.1, Copper Development Association, ASM Handbook, AZoM, Accuratus.
