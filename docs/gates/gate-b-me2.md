Each spec is in `specs/<id>.md` with status `draft`. For each one, check that the learning goal suits the 2024 Ext 2 course, the parameter ranges suit a classroom, the acceptance numbers look right, and the prompts make students think (guide §10). Every acceptance number has its formula and hand working in the spec. Independent scripts recomputed every expected value from closed forms (0 failures). The time-based values were also checked against an RK4 run at dt = 1/240 s, the polynomial roots against a Durand–Kerner run, the integrals against Simpson's rule, and the logic table by brute force over ranges of integers and reals.

The three argand specs use the exact keys of the `argand` meta (merged to `main` in #13), and their 29 checks (127 values) also pass when run through that engine's own model, with its presets and parameter syncing. The two fnplot specs (P2) use the exact keys of the `fnplot` meta in PR #28 (the fnplot engine), and their 13 checks (32 values) pass through that engine's model too. No space3d or particle2d meta had been pushed when the P1 specs were written, so those specs list their keys under "Engine change requests" as keys to reconcile.

## P0

- **me2-argand-explorer** (argand / operations). Goal: multiplying by z₂ rotates by arg z₂ and scales by |z₂|; de Moivre as a spiral of powers; the roots of zⁿ = w form a regular n-gon. Key numbers: (2 + i)(1 + i) = 1 + 3i, modulus √10 = 3.16228, argument 1.24905 = 0.46365 + π/4; (1 + 3i)/(1 + i) = 2 + i; (−1 + i)⁵ = 4 − 4i, argument −π/4; principal root of z⁴ = −4 is 1 + i; i² = −1 with argument π (not −π); (2 + i)i = −1 + 2i, same modulus √5.

## P1

- **me2-3d-lines-spheres** (space3d / lines-spheres). Goal: vector equation of a line; classify two lines as intersecting, parallel or skew; line–sphere intersections from a quadratic in λ and its discriminant. Key numbers (line 1 = (1, 2, 3) + λ(1, −1, 2)): skew with (2, 0, 1) + μ(1, 1, 1), distance 9/√14 = 2.40535, angle 61.8745°; intersecting with (0, −1, 3) + μ(1, 1, 1) at (2, 1, 5), λ = 1, μ = 2; parallel with v = −2u, distance √(59/6) = 3.13581; coincident; sphere centre (1, 1, 1), R = 3 cut by (1, 0, 5) + λ(0, 1, −1) at λ = 1, 4 (Δ = 36, chord √18); tangent at λ = 3 (Δ = 0); R = 2 misses (Δ = −4).
- **me2-loci-painter** (argand / loci). Goal: read |z − a| as distance and arg(z − a) as direction from a; sketch circles, discs, bisectors, half-planes, rays and sectors; included (solid) and excluded (dashed, hollow) boundaries; two conditions together; test a point. Key numbers: z = 3 + i is on |z − (2 + i)| = 1, so it fails `<` and passes `≤`; arg(z − 1) = π/4 contains 2 + i but not −i (argument −3π/4); the midpoint 2 + i of 1 and 3 + 2i is √2 from both; the default region |z − (1 + i)| ≤ 2 and arg z ≥ 0 contains 1 + 2i but not 2 − 0.5i (argument −0.24498); the ray arg z = π/4 meets |z − 2| = 2 at 2 + 2i but not at the excluded vertex 0; along the cut arg = π, −2 is on the ray θ = 180°, is excluded from arg z < −90° (dashed edge) and included in arg z ≥ 90° (solid edge). Requested from the engine: extremes of |z| and arg z on a circle (for |z − (3 + 4i)| = 2: 3 to 7, and 0.51578 to 1.33881).
- **me2-resisted-motion** (particle2d / resisted-1d). ## P2

- **me2-conjugate-roots** (argand / polynomial-roots). Goal: real coefficients force non-real roots into conjugate pairs (each pair gives a real quadratic factor); a real cubic has a real root; one non-real coefficient breaks the mirror; sums and products of roots follow the coefficients. Key numbers: z³ − 3z² + 4z − 2 = (z − 1)(z² − 2z + 2) has roots 1, 1 ± i, sum 3, product 2; z² + 2z + 5 has roots −1 ± 2i; z² − 2i has roots ±(1 + i) (not a pair), product −2i; z⁴ + 4 has roots ±1 ± i, product 4; z⁴ − 1 has 2 real roots; 2z² − 4z + 4 has sum 2 and product 2 (= −c₁/c₂ and c₀/c₂); z³ + 1 has roots −1 and 0.5 ± 0.86603i; z² + 2z + 5 + i has roots −1.24810 + 2.01533i and −0.75190 − 2.01533i.
- **me2-integration-by-parts** (fnplot / by-parts). Goal: ∫ u dv + ∫ v du = u(b)v(b) − u(a)v(a), seen as two signed areas (measured as x goes from a to b) cut off by the curve (u(x), v(x)), with u and v typed as expressions; reduction formulas applied step by step. Key numbers: ∫₀¹ x eˣ dx = e − (e − 1) = 1; ∫₁² ln x dx = 2 ln 2 − 1 = 0.38629; ∫₀^(π/2) x cos x dx = π/2 − 1 = 0.57080; past π/2, ∫₀³ x cos x dx = 3 sin 3 − (1 − cos 3) = −1.56663 (signed, since sin x turns back); ∫₀¹ tan⁻¹ x dx = π/4 − (ln 2)/2 = 0.43882; ∫₀^(π/2) sin⁵ x dx = 8/15 and sin⁶ gives 5π/32 = 0.49087; ∫₀¹ x³ eˣ dx = 6 − 2e = 0.56344.
- **me2-partial-fractions** (fnplot / partial-fractions). Goal: decompose fractions with distinct linear factors (dividing first when improper); the pieces add back to the graph; a zero coefficient means a cancelled factor (a hole); each piece integrates to a logarithm; repeated factors as an extension. Key numbers (coefficients in order of root): (5x + 1)/((x − 1)(x + 2)) = 3/(x + 2) + 2/(x − 1); (3x² + x − 1)/(x(x − 1)(x + 1)) gives 1/2, 1, 3/2; (x − 1)/((x − 1)(x + 2)) gives 1 and 0; (x³ + 2)/(x² − 1) = x − (1/2)/(x + 1) + (3/2)/(x − 1); (x + 3)/(x − 1)² = 1/(x − 1) + 4/(x − 1)². Requested from the engine: an integral readout (default on [2, 4]: 3.41362) and x² + q factors.
- **me2-proof-pictures** (custom). Goal: AM ≥ GM (and HM ≤ GM ≤ AM ≤ QM) from a semicircle picture and a square picture, with equality exactly when a = b; converse, contrapositive, inverse and negation with counterexamples; the structure of a proof by contradiction (√2 and log₂ 3 irrational, with proof text written for the page). Key numbers: a = 4, b = 1 gives HM 1.6, GM 2, AM 2.5, QM √8.5 = 2.91548, gap 0.5; a = b = 3 gives all four equal to 3; a = 3, b = 1 gives 16 = 12 + 4; "if x > 2 then x² > 4" has a false converse (x = −3) and a true contrapositive; "every prime n > 1 is odd" is false, so its negation is true.
