# Gate B: Physics specs (part A: pages on shared engines)

One line per spec: id, learning goal, key acceptance numbers. Every number has its formula and hand working in the spec's Acceptance section and was rechecked with a short Node calculation. Part B (custom-engine pages and P2) is listed in its own PR.

Physics presets use g = 9.8 m/s²; the Engineering listing preset `eng-horizontal` uses g = 10 m/s².

## P0

- **[phy-double-slit](../../specs/phy-double-slit.md)** (wave / interference; keys match the `wave` engine's meta): bright fringes at whole-number path differences; d sin θ = mλ and Δy ≈ λL/d; gratings give sharper, wider-spread maxima. Checks: 600 nm, d 0.25 mm, L 2 m gives θ₁ = 0.13751°, λL/d = 4.80 mm, exact y₁ = 4.80001 mm, central fringe 4.80 mm; 450 nm gives 3.60 mm; 650 nm, d 0.50 mm, L 3 m gives 3.90 mm; grating 600 lines/mm at 500 nm gives θ₁ = 17.458°, y₁ = 0.629 m (small-angle 0.600 m), 3 orders, central width 12.0 mm with 100 slits lit and 1.20 mm with 1000; at 700 nm θ₁ = 24.835°, 2 orders; single slit a = 0.05 mm: first minimum 0.6876°, central width 48.0 mm.
- **[phy-projectile-lab](../../specs/phy-projectile-lab.md)** (particle2d / projectile; keys match the `particle2d` engine's meta; presets `me1-vectors` and `me2-drag` for the Maths listings): independent horizontal and vertical motion; T, H, R from the uniform-acceleration equations, including launches from a height; effect of air resistance. Checks: 20 m/s at 45° gives R = 40.816 m, H = 10.204 m at 1.443 s, T = 2.886 s; 30° and 60° both give R = 35.348 m; horizontal launch at 15 m/s from 20 m gives T = 2.020 s, R = 30.305 m, impact 24.84 m/s; 20 m/s at 30° from 10 m gives R = 48.08 m; Moon (g 1.6) R = 250 m; after 240 steps x = 17.32 m, y = 5.1 m, vᵧ = 0.2 m/s; linear drag (k/m = 0.1 s⁻¹) H = 9.318 m, R = 34.10 m; `me2-drag` (linear kv, m 1, k 0.2, 30 m/s at 45°, the Ext 2 closed-form case): top at 1.799 s, H = 17.94 m, T = 3.842 s, R = 56.88 m; `quadratic-drag` (kv², k 0.02) launched vertically at 30 m/s: H = 26.07 m, T = 4.623 s, impact 17.81 m/s.

## Engine keys

Specs on engines that have published `meta` use it exactly: wave (`phy-double-slit`, `phy-wave-machine`), statics (`phy-inclined-plane`), statsim (`phy-radioactive-decay`) and particle2d (`phy-projectile-lab`, `phy-charged-particles`, `phy-circular-motion`, `phy-collisions`, `phy-motion-graphs`, `phy-orbit-lab`, `phy-relative-velocity`). For each, every param, preset value, output, enum value and range was checked against the meta, and every expected value was run through the engine's own model functions and passes. The other engines (fnplot, field2d, rays, circuit) had not published `meta` when these were written, so those specs list their keys under "Engine change requests: keys to reconcile with engine meta"; the page builder reconciles them (since #23, `npm test` checks a spec's keys against its engine only once the page is in review).
