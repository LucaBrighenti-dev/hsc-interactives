# Gate B: Physics specs (part A: pages on shared engines)

One line per spec: id, learning goal, key acceptance numbers. Every number has its formula and hand working in the spec's Acceptance section and was rechecked with a short Node calculation. Part B (custom-engine pages and P2) is listed in its own PR.

Physics presets use g = 9.8 m/s²; the Engineering listing preset `eng-horizontal` uses g = 10 m/s².

## P0

- **[phy-double-slit](../../specs/phy-double-slit.md)** (wave / interference; keys match the `wave` engine's meta): bright fringes at whole-number path differences; d sin θ = mλ and Δy ≈ λL/d; gratings give sharper, wider-spread maxima. Checks: 600 nm, d 0.25 mm, L 2 m gives θ₁ = 0.13751°, λL/d = 4.80 mm, exact y₁ = 4.80001 mm, central fringe 4.80 mm; 450 nm gives 3.60 mm; 650 nm, d 0.50 mm, L 3 m gives 3.90 mm; grating 600 lines/mm at 500 nm gives θ₁ = 17.458°, y₁ = 0.629 m (small-angle 0.600 m), 3 orders, central width 12.0 mm with 100 slits lit and 1.20 mm with 1000; at 700 nm θ₁ = 24.835°, 2 orders; single slit a = 0.05 mm: first minimum 0.6876°, central width 48.0 mm.
- **[phy-projectile-lab](../../specs/phy-projectile-lab.md)** (particle2d / projectile; presets `me1-vectors`, `me2-drag` for the Maths listings): independent horizontal and vertical motion; T, H, R from the uniform-acceleration equations, including launches from a height; effect of air resistance. Checks: 20 m/s at 45° gives R = 40.816 m, H = 10.204 m, T = 2.886 s; 30° and 60° both give R = 35.348 m; horizontal launch at 15 m/s from 20 m gives T = 2.020 s, R = 30.305 m, impact 24.84 m/s; 20 m/s at 30° from 10 m gives R = 48.08 m; Moon (g 1.6) R = 250 m; after 240 steps vᵧ = 0.2 m/s, x = 17.32 m; linear drag (k/m = 0.1 s⁻¹) H = 9.318 m, R = 34.10 m; `me2-drag` (linear kv, m 1, k 0.2, 30 m/s at 45°, the Ext 2 closed-form case): H = 17.94 m, T = 3.842 s, R = 56.88 m, landing vₓ = 9.838 m/s; `quadratic-drag` (kv², k 0.02) launched vertically at 30 m/s: H = 26.07 m, T = 4.623 s, impact 17.81 m/s.

- **[phy-collisions](../../specs/phy-collisions.md)** (particle2d / collisions): momentum conserved in every collision (by components in 2D); KE conserved only if elastic. ## Engine keys

`phy-double-slit` (wave) and `phy-inclined-plane` (statics) use their engines' published meta, and every check in them was also run through that engine's own model functions and passes. The other engines had not published `meta` when these were written, so those specs list their param and output keys under "Engine change requests: keys to reconcile with engine meta". The page builder (or the engine owner) reconciles them. Note for the orchestrator: `npm test` checks a draft spec's acceptance keys against its engine's `meta` once that engine exists in `site/assets/engines/`, so an engine PR whose keys differ from its specs will fail that test until one side is renamed.
